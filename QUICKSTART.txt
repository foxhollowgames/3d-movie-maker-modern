3D Movie Maker Modern - Fox Hollow Games distribution
Based on 3DMMEx 0.7.0, 3DMMForever, and Microsoft 3D Movie Maker.
This is a community project. Microsoft does not sponsor this distribution.

WINDOWS 10 / 11
1. Extract the complete ZIP to a folder that you can write to.
2. Open "Start 3D Movie Maker.cmd" or "3dmovie.exe".
3. Follow the on-screen introduction to enter the movie studio.

Use the x64 download for Intel or AMD computers.
Use the ARM64 download for Windows on ARM computers.
Keep the "Microsoft Kids" folder beside the executable.
No original CD, installer, or administrator access is required.

DISPLAY SIZE
The app starts in fullscreen on every launch.
Press Alt/Option-Enter to switch fullscreen mode.
Resize or maximize the window in windowed mode to fit your screen.
The picture keeps its original 4:3 proportions.
A dark theater texture fills unused space on widescreen and portrait displays.
Fullscreen preserves your desktop resolution, including 1080p, 1440p, and 4K.
The original artwork is enlarged. Its source detail does not change.

SAMPLE MOVIES
Open a movie from "Microsoft Kids/Users/McZee" in the application.
Use the Play control to play it.
Save your own movies in a writable folder, such as Documents.

macOS (APPLE SILICON)
Extract the ZIP. Open "3D Movie Maker.app".
The application is not notarized. macOS can block its first launch.
Use the macOS Privacy & Security controls to review the blocked app.
Cutscene playback is not implemented in the upstream macOS version.
Alt/Option-Enter switches the SDL version between windowed and fullscreen mode.

LINUX
Build instructions are in README.md. No portable Linux download is supplied.
